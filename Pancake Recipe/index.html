Pancake Recipe:

Pancakes are also known as hotcakes, gridlecake, or flapjacks depending on the country.They are often flat round cakes mostly known in England and America, but can also be thick and fluffy like in Scotland, and Japan. They can be served sweet using maple syrup, jam, chocoloate or honey. They can also be served as a savoury treat.

Today we will show you how to make an American style pancake.

Ingredients:

.Dry Ingredients:

  • 1 cup (120g) all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt

.Wet Ingredients:

  • 1 cup (240ml) milk
  • 2 tablespoons of metled butter
  • 1 large egg
  • 1/2 teaspoon of vanilla extract or cinammon (optional)

Steps:

  1. Add all the dry Ingredients in a medium bowl.
  2. In a seprate bowl, whisk together all the wet ingredients.
  3. Pour the wet ingredients into the bowl with the dry ingredients and stir until just combined.
  4. Heat a non-stick skillet or griddle over medium heat and lightly grease it with butter or oil.
  5. Pour 1/4 cup of batter onto the skillet for each pancake.
  6. Cook until bubbles form on the surface of the pancake and the edges look set, about 2-3 minutes.
  7. After bubbles start forming, flip the pancake using a spatula and cook it for another 1-2 minutes.
  8. Remove the pancake from the skillet and keep it warm. Repeat with the remaining batter.
  9. Serve the pancakes with your favorite toppings such as maple syrup, fresh fruits, whipped cream, honey or nuts.
